Email / Text Alerts

Runs entirely in your browser via EmailJS — free, no backend. Only fires while this page is open and auto-refreshing (every 5 min). For a text instead of an email, put your carrier's email-to-SMS address in the notify field (e.g. 5551234567@vtext.com for Verizon, @txt.att.net AT&T, @tmomail.net T-Mobile).

Your EmailJS template needs variables {{to_email}}, {{subject}}, and {{message}}. See the README for full setup steps.

Data: National Weather Service (station observations, US only) and Open-Meteo (best-match, NOAA GFS, DWD ICON, ECMWF IFS, ECCC GEM, UK Met Office, Météo-France, JMA). Track/venue search uses OpenStreetMap Nominatim (© OpenStreetMap contributors) alongside Open-Meteo's geocoder. Radar via Windy.com. Density altitude uses the standard pressure-altitude + ISA lapse-rate approximation; air density ratio comes from actual station pressure, temperature and humidity via the ideal gas law; the correction factor uses SAE J1349 (99 kPa / 298 K reference). These are field-reference numbers — cross-check against your own NHRA/SAE calculator before making a tuning call.
